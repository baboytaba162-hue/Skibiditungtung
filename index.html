To my beautiful love,

Hi, my beautiful love. I love you very, very much. Every week and month that goes by, I fall in love with you more and more. No one could replace you or be more beautiful to me. Having you beside me feels like a blessing. Ever since you came into my life, my days have been better. I wait for your replies; you make me smile and feel happy and joyful. Words can’t express how grateful I am.

If being beautiful made you rich, you’d be a billionaire by now—that’s how beautiful you are. Don’t listen to what other people say. Be yourself; that’s what makes you special. You don’t have to hide things from me. I’ll accept you no matter what.

These four months have been the happiest I’ve had because you’re in my life. If people were stars, you’d be the shiniest one. You shine like no other, and no one compares to you. Learning new things about you is so much fun—what you like, what you listen to, and what you do when you’re bored. Your chitchats are fun to listen to; just listening to you is fun already.

Spending time with you is the best. Spending time with my beautiful, cute, adorable, smart, hardworking, funny, smol love is always worth it—no time wasted. I’ll always be your biggest fan. I’d be there for you whenever you needed me because I care about you extremely much. Your feelings are important to me. Whether it’s a small problem or a big one, tell me about it. I won’t overreact or say you’re exaggerating.

No one would probably know how much I truly love you. I’m sorry if I sometimes ignore you or don’t talk to you and make you overthink. It doesn’t mean I have a problem with you; I might just be prioritizing something first. It wouldn’t be me if I didn’t talk to you or annoy you! I’m sorry if my actions hurt you. I’m not always good at expressing myself, and I want to keep getting better.

One very, very important thing: please don’t hide how you feel. If something is hurting you or causing you a problem, tell me. I don’t want you to keep it all inside.

Once again, you’re very beautiful, and I’ll remind you whenever I can. I’m being completely truthful: you’re beautiful with or without makeup, stressed or relaxed, lazy or hardworking. In my eyes, you’re incredible. Seeing you work hard makes me proud and happy, knowing the girl I love is working toward her future. Seeing you having fun makes me feel calm, because I never want you to have a bad day.

The gifts and food I give you aren’t just ordinary gifts. I think about what you might like. I ask about your favorite food, anime, or drink because I want to choose something that feels special to you.

I love you so much. ❤️
